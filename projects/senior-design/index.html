Senior Design

Automated Isocyanate Agitation System

Senior design project in progress for Atmus Filtration Technologies. The goal of the project is to automate a polyurethane agitation process that previously required manual rotation on a scheduled basis.

Project Overview

This system is being designed to rotate an agitator automatically, complete a required number of turns, and use encoder feedback to confirm that the expected motion occurred.

My Role

  • PLC programming and sequence development
  • HMI screen development
  • Motor and gearbox selection
  • Encoder integration
  • Electrical enclosure layout
  • Power distribution and wiring design
  • Controls troubleshooting and system integration

System Requirements

  • Complete 10 agitator rotations per 24 hours
  • Confirm completed rotations using encoder feedback
  • Operate at approximately 50 RPM
  • Provide approximately 10 in-lb of required torque
  • Provide manual controls through the HMI
  • Use a compact industrial control enclosure

Controls & Automation

PLC Logic Development

The controls system uses a CLICK Plus PLC and C-more HMI to manage the automatic sequence, operator commands, interlocks, motor control, timing, and encoder confirmation. Initial PLC logic was developed to establish the sequence structure and rotation-confirmation process before final system implementation and testing.

Early PLC logic development for the automated agitation sequence.

HMI Development

The C-more HMI provides operators with manual controls, real-time equipment status, and clear feedback from the agitation system. Separate screens were developed for automatic operation, manual control, navigation, and I/O status to keep the interface simple and easy to use.

Mechanical & Electrical Design

Motor & Gearbox Selection

Motor and gearbox options were evaluated based on torque, speed, physical size, control method, and compatibility with the available 24 VDC control system.

Electrical Design

The project includes enclosure layout, circuit protection, terminal blocks, power distribution, Ethernet communication, motor wiring, operator controls, and documentation for installation and troubleshooting.
