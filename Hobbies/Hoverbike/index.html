CONCEPT DESIGN

HOVERBIKE CONCEPT

A personal design project exploring the packaging, structure, propulsion layout, and overall feasibility of a compact hoverbike.

TYPE Personal Design
FOCUS Mechanical Packaging
TOOLS CAD & Rendering
STATUS Concept Development

01

THE IDEA

Why a Hoverbike?

This project started as a way to explore what a small personal flying vehicle could look like if the mechanical systems were packaged into something closer to a motorcycle than a traditional aircraft.

02

DESIGN

Packaging & Layout

A major part of the design is fitting the propulsion system, structure, rider position, and controls into a compact layout while keeping the overall form practical and balanced.
