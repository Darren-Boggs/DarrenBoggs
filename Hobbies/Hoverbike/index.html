Concept Design

Hoverbike Concept

A personal design project focused on developing a compact hoverbike concept through CAD, mechanical packaging, component layout, and repeated design revisions.

Project Overview

The idea behind this project was to explore how a small personal flying vehicle could be packaged into something closer to a motorcycle than a traditional aircraft. The design has changed several times as I have worked through the frame layout, propulsion placement, rider position, and overall proportions.

Early Design

The early versions were mostly focused on figuring out the overall shape and where the major components could fit. These models helped me work through the basic proportions and identify areas that needed to change.

Design Development

As the design progressed, I started focusing more on mechanical packaging, structural layout, component placement, and making the concept feel more like something that could actually be built.

Key Design Areas

  • Overall frame and chassis layout
  • Propulsion system placement
  • Rider position and packaging
  • Mechanical component layout
  • CAD modeling and design revisions
  • Structural and fabrication considerations

Current Status

The design is still evolving. I continue to revise the layout and proportions as I work through different ideas and improve the overall concept.
