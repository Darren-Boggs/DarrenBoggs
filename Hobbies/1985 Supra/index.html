Automotive Project

1985 Toyota Celica

My first car and a long-term personal project. We have been through a lot together over the years.

The Day I Bought Her

This is where the story started. I bought the Celica as my first car, beginning a project that would continue to change and evolve over the years.

The First Engine

The first engine became one of my earliest major learning experiences with the car. The old cracked head made for a very long period of "why wont it start?"

The Second Time I Bought Her

After being separated from the car for a period of time, I had the opportunity to buy it back. Bringing the Celica home again started a new chapter of the project and gave me the chance to continue rebuilding and improving it.

Glass Sunroof

The coolest part by far is the sunroof. Gaining inspiration from an old forum with a single photo as refernce, I retrofitted a 2001 Honda Prelude glass sunrrof.This involved precise measuring, fitting, fabrication, and body work to integrate the new roof into the car.

Paint & Wrap

The exterior has seen better days but soon I plan to paint and wrap it with the pictured "renderings"
